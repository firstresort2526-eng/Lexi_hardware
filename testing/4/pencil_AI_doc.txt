Brief description of how it will work:
1. Raspberry pi camera takes a picture
2. Resize it to 640*640
3. Send it to SAM3 to draw bounding box of a pencil
4. Crop that image so that you only get the pencil part
5. Use a custom tensorflow model to get the coordinates of the pencil. (0,0) would be the top left of the cropped image (only the pencil part)
6. Calculate the coordinate in terms of 640*640 image
7. Rescale it to original size
8. Use a OCR to get the characters & their coordinates
9. Use simple math (I mean, simple) to calculate find the character nearest to pencil tip coordinate
